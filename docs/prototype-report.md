# Disposable UX prototype report

Route: `/prototype?variant=<guided|dashboard|data|role>`

The route is visibly labeled disposable and does not alter production routes or domain architecture.

## A — Guided workflow

Best for the selected primary audience and manual entry. It reduces costly mistakes through ordered stages and review-before-solve. Trade-off: experienced operators need more navigation.

## B — Operations dashboard

Keeps model health, warnings, solver status, quality, and diagnostics on one surface. Trade-off: higher initial density and weaker first-time guidance.

## C — Data-first workspace

Prioritizes Excel, JSON, validation, model summaries, and export integrity. Trade-off: domain concepts may be less approachable to nontechnical managers.

## D — Role-based entry

Explains distinct paths for managers, scheduling staff, instructors, technical users, and evaluators. Trade-off: role selection adds a step and some roles are not full editors in the approved scope.

## Selected production direction

The conversation’s approved primary audience and manual-first workflow make Guided Workflow the production default. The other three variants remain comparison prototypes only.
